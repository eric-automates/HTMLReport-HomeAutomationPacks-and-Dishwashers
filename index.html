Part 1: Modular Open-Source Home Monitoring

Deploying twenty to fifty sensing nodes across a residential infrastructure exposes the shortcomings of closed consumer hardware. True infrastructure-grade deployment requires a standardized base compute engine supporting hot-swappable daughterboards without cloud intermediaries.

Evaluation Criteria (125-Point Scale)

  • Hardware Modularity (30 points): Accommodation of interchangeable daughterboards and digital breakout buses.
  • Local Autonomy (25 points): Verification of zero mandatory cloud connectivity and zero subscription gating.
  • Build Quality (25 points): Production quality of enclosures and power topology.
  • Cost-Effectiveness & Value (25 points): Financial viability for multi-node deployment, scaling efficiency, and average cost per completed node.
  • Community Support (20 points): Velocity of upstream repository commits and documentation depth.

Part 2: Precision-Engineered Automatic Dishwashers

Precision dishwashing addresses challenges through two primary design philosophies: Category A (matched-rack commercial adaptations) and Category B (closed-loop, generic dishwashers with active process verification).

Evaluation Criteria (125-Point Scale)

  • Cleaning Efficacy (35 points): Verified mechanical removal of baked-on food soils.
  • Coverage & Sensing (25 points): Precision of fluid delivery and optical soil-sensing suites.
  • Build Quality (25 points): Tub materials, brushless motors, and filtration design.
  • Cost-Effectiveness & Value (25 points): Feature-to-price ratio, long-term ROI, and acquisition cost relative to segment peers.
  • Availability (15 points): Utility compatibility and domestic technician networks.
